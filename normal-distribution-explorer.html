Symmetric curves

In a normal distribution the mean, median and mode are the same number. Moving the mean slides the whole curve without changing its shape.

Skewed curves

Drag the mode away from the mean and a tail grows on the other side. The mean is pulled furthest toward the tail, the mode stays at the peak, and the median always sits between them.

Rule of thumb

For moderately skewed data, the gap between mean and mode is roughly three times the gap between mean and median. That is why the median slider moves in much smaller steps than the mode slider.

Normalizing

Hover over the curve to read any point as a z-score. For a symmetric curve, z = 1 always has 84.1% of values below it. Once the curve is skewed, that share changes.
